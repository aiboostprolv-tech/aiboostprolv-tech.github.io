What an AI consultation covers

  • Where AI saves time in your business. We look at the questions customers ask every day and the tasks you repeat, and find what can be automated.
  • Which tools are worth it and which are not. An honest recommendation, including when AI is the wrong answer.
  • Conversational AI. Whether a chatbot on your website, WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger makes sense, and what it should and should not answer.
  • Visibility in AI search. How your business appears in Google and in answers from AI assistants, and what to fix on your website.
