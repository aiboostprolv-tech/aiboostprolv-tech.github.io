  1. Free call. Tell me which task repeats every day or week and how much time it takes.

When you don't need automation

If a task happens once a month and takes 10 minutes, automation will pay off very slowly, and I will tell you so. It pays off best for work that repeats daily, where mistakes cost money, or where a customer is waiting for a reply.

Frequently asked questions

How much does business process automation cost?

At AiBoost Pro a simple automation (one flow) costs from €250 one-off excl. VAT, medium €500–1,200, complex from €1,500 by scope. Maintenance from €49/month if needed. The first call is free.

Which tools do you use?

We pick them for your process: often Google Sheets, email and connector tools such as Make, Zapier or n8n are enough. If text or documents need to be understood, we add AI.

Usually not. Automation connects what you already use, as long as the software can connect to other tools.

What if the automation stops working?

With maintenance I monitor the flows and fix them when a tool changes. Without maintenance, fixes are quoted separately.

What happens to my data?
