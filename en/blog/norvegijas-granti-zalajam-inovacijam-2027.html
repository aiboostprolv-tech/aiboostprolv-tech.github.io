Norway grants for green innovation in 2027: what we know

18.11.2026 · Reading time 3 min · Kristīne Vasiļjeva

Short answer: LIAA has announced that three Norway grant calls for green innovation will open in 2027. The programme's total funding is EUR 32.54 million: EUR 27.69 million from Norway grants and EUR 4.85 million from Latvia's state co-financing. Exact deadlines have not yet been published.

Three calls in 2027

CallGrant sizePurposeCall budget
Q1 2027EUR 500,000–3,000,000Scaling up green innovations already on the marketEUR 9.37 million
Q2 2027EUR 100,000–499,999Developing and bringing green products and technologies to marketEUR 12.53 million
Q4 2027EUR 50,000–99,999Prototypes; half of the funds for women entrepreneurs and start-upsEUR 2 million

What to do now

Application deadlines and conditions are not yet known. Before applying, check the LIAA website liaa.gov.lv. If your project relates to green innovation, you can contact us free of charge to discuss whether it might qualify. We cannot promise that a grant will be awarded.

Frequently asked questions

Is the application open yet?

LIAA plans the calls for 2027. LIAA will publish the exact deadlines, application procedure and conditions on its website.

How large will the grant be?

It depends on the call: EUR 50,000–99,999 for prototypes, EUR 100,000–499,999 for developing and marketing green products and technologies, and EUR 500,000–3,000,000 for scaling up.

Who administers the programme?

LIAA administers the programme together with Innovation Norway. The programme agreement was signed by Latvia's Ministry of Finance and Norway's Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Source: LIAA announcement, published on 16.10.2026 by LVportāls. Programme conditions are set by the relevant legislation and may change.
